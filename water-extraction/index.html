Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. New Haven Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout New Haven using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

Our Water Extraction Process

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle water extraction from start to finish:

1

Fast Arrival and Setup

The crew gets to you fast, with portable and truck-mounted extractors ready to go to work at once. We assess the situation and begin extraction shortly after arrival.

2

Removing the Standing Water

We use truck-mounted extraction to clear high volumes of water standing on carpet, flooring and surfaces. For crawl spaces and basements holding deep water, we switch to submersible pumps.

3

Detailed Moisture Extraction

With specialty tools we draw moisture out of upholstery, carpet and pad, wall cavities and other spots that are hard to reach. Your property dries sooner when more of the moisture comes out through mechanical extraction.

4

Moving On to Drying

When no more water can be extracted, commercial air movers and dehumidifiers are set in place to dry the structure. We take moisture readings at this point, which become the baseline for tracking how drying proceeds.

When to Call for Water Extraction in New Haven

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Carpeting so wet it squelches when you step on it or has puddles on top
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn
  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
