Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain

How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
